Pocket Architect

Architectural drawing prompts for practice

Pocket Architect is a free random drawing-prompt generator for architecture students, hobbyists, and designers. It gives you a building type, a technical drawing perspective, and a site placement so you can start sketching instead of choosing what to draw.

How to use the drawing prompt generator

  1. Choose a skill level. Beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels control the difficulty of the constraints.
  2. Generate a brief. Combine the three cards into one architectural sketch: building, perspective, and placement.
  3. Set a timer and draw. Use a 15, 30, or 45 minute session, or choose Free draw for an open-ended exercise.

Example architectural sketch exercises

Beginner exercise

Draw a garden shed in elevation on a flat open field. Focus on proportion, a clear roof pitch, and a clean horizon.

Intermediate exercise

Draw a greenhouse in axonometric view on a riverbank. Show the light frame, glazing, and how the building meets the water.

Advanced exercise

Draw an opera house from a three-point perspective in a dense city intersection. Make the massing and overlapping context readable.

Architectural drawing prompt FAQ

What is an architectural drawing prompt?

An architectural drawing prompt is a small design brief that gives you something specific to sketch. Pocket Architect combines a building type, a technical drawing perspective, and a site placement.

Is Pocket Architect free?

Yes. Pocket Architect is a free, local-first browser tool with no account or server required.

Who is Pocket Architect for?

It is designed for architecture students, hobbyists, and designers who want a quick, structured drawing exercise without spending time deciding what to draw.

What perspectives can I practice?

Prompts include elevations, plans, one-point and two-point perspective, sections, axonometric and isometric drawings, three-point perspective, aerial views, and exploded axons.
